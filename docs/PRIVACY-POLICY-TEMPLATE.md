MediTrip India is a general hospital directory and travel planning application. It is not a medical diagnosis, treatment, emergency dispatch or booking service. It does not operate an AI service.

## Information on your device

The Android app stores selected language/theme, saved public hospital/hotel IDs, checklist completion and amounts you enter in a local trip budget within its private WebView storage. This information is not uploaded to the publisher. The app does not request name, diagnosis, reports, passport, location, camera, microphone, contacts or payment credentials. Saved trip choices may still reveal interests in healthcare; treat exported plans as private.

## Audio

Text-to-speech uses the phone's speech engine and an installed offline voice. The app does not send text to a publisher server or select a network voice. Speech engines are separate system applications. Voice downloads or the system speech settings may use that provider's connectivity and terms. The web edition can use only voices reported as local by the browser; availability depends on the platform.

## Export, import and sharing

You may save a JSON plan using Android's document picker or share a text summary using an app you choose. The selected storage/share provider receives that file or text at your instruction; its privacy terms apply. Imports are read only after you select a file. No access to other files is requested. The publisher does not receive exports.

## External sites

Official provider, map, visa, email and phone actions open external browser/system applications. The destination may receive ordinary network information and any information you enter there. No GPS is requested; Maps may ask for location independently. The Android app itself has no INTERNET permission, advertising, analytics or trackers.

For the optional hosted web edition, the hosting provider receives normal HTTP requests/IP addresses and may retain server access logs. This policy's no-network Android statement does not apply to the host. Fill in your host and any changed behaviour before publication.

## Deletion and control

Use Settings > Clear my plan to delete saved journey/checklist/budget within the app. Language/theme preferences remain. Android Clear Storage or uninstall removes local app data; web users can clear site storage. Files already exported/shared remain in their chosen destination and must be removed there. No automatic app backup/device transfer is enabled in this Android project.

## Retention, changes and contact

Local entries remain until you clear them or app/site storage is removed. There is no publisher account or cloud account deletion process because no account service exists. We will update this policy if the service changes. Contact the publisher at the email above for questions or corrections.
