# Play submission checklist

This package does not submit anything or guarantee store approval. Requirements can change; review current Play Console guidance at submission.

1. Complete publisher name, support email and a public, accessible HTTPS privacy policy URL. Edit the supplied template to match your release. Use the policy both in Play Console and `web/config.js`.
2. Produce your **signed release Android App Bundle** with your upload key, not a debug APK. Keep keystore/passwords outside the package. Confirm package ID availability, versionCode, app name and trademark rights.
3. Test on actual Android devices before collecting **real screenshots** (minimum number/size per Console). Feature graphic supplied is original 1024×500 PNG. Do not use design mockups as evidence of tested screens.
4. Complete Data safety accurately for your app as shipped. This source has no publisher collection/ads/analytics; local planning and user-initiated export/share are described in the policy. Adding SDKs, hosting or services changes your answer. Do not blindly copy declarations.
5. Review and complete the **Health apps declaration**, medical-information disclaimer, content rating and target audience as applicable. This product is directory/travel planning, not diagnosis or regulated treatment. Official-source labels must not become false verified/accreditation badges.
6. Confirm provider data freshness and professional translation quality. Fill missing support contact; add licensed photos only with permission. Search-summary records should be confirmed by directly visiting the official branch pages.
7. Set pricing free, countries of distribution and review app access (no login). Follow any closed-testing/production-access requirements shown for your developer account. Reviewers must be able to use offline functions.
8. Use an internal test release, then closed testing as needed, then submit. Approval and account-specific gates are controlled by Google.
