# Artwork and screenshots

- `feature-graphic-en.png`: original 1024×500 graphic suitable for the feature-graphic slot after publisher review.
- `web/assets/icon-512.png`: original app icon. Android launchers are generated in mipmap folders.
- `docs/previews/*.png`: actual headless Chrome captures of the shipped web UI. They are web previews, **not Android device captures**. Full-page images demonstrate layout; their aspect ratio is unsuitable for a Play phone screenshot slot. Use actual Android phone captures for the final listing.
- No provider photos/logos or fabricated clinician portraits.
- Font binaries in web/assets/fonts: official Noto project, SIL OFL 1.1 included. Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts . Only CSS family aliases changed; font binaries were not modified.
